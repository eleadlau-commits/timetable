Timetable

Connect your timetable

Your university can export your timetable as a personal calendar link. You switch the export on once, copy the link, and paste it here. After that the app keeps itself up to date.

  1. Open your timetable on the university website and sign in. This is often easiest on a laptop.
  2. Find the export option. It may be called Export, Subscribe, Sync to calendar, iCal or Add to Google/Outlook, and is often behind a calendar or share icon.
  3. Confirm the export when it asks you to. This creates your personal link.
  4. Copy the link. It usually starts with webcal:// or https:// and often ends in .ics. On a phone, press and hold the link and choose Copy link.
  5. Paste it below and tap Load timetable.

Link received. Check it, then tap Load timetable.

Keep this link private, because anyone who has it can see your timetable. The app stores it only on this device.

Other ways to get the link in

Copied it on a computer? Open this app on the computer as well, paste the link into the box above, then tap the button below and scan the code with your phone's camera. You can also email the link to yourself and copy it from the email on your phone.
Copied it on a computer? Email or message the link to yourself, open it on your phone, then press and hold the link and choose Copy.
On Android? Once the app is on your home screen, you can tap Share on the link in any app and pick Timetable.
Got a file instead of a link? If the export downloaded an .ics file, import it here. A file won't update by itself, so re-import it when your timetable changes.

Settings

Advanced: personal relay

Some university servers stop web apps from reading the link directly. If loading says it was blocked, set up the free relay in relay/README.md and paste its address here.
